## `swm/OGBMaze-v0`

stable-envs: `PointmazeLargeNavigate`, `PointmazeLargeStitch` - 5 swm leaves, 2 mapped, 2 unsupported, 1 dead_upstream

> Classified in ob_type='pixels'. UPSTREAM FINDING: the registered id swm/OGBMaze-v0 defaults to ob_type='states', and in THAT configuration all five leaves move 0 pixels -- swm's compile_model() rebuilds ogbench's `custom_renderer` only for 'pixels', so a states-mode render keeps drawing the pre-recompile model even though mat_rgba provably changed. Our port reproduces the OGBench pixel renderer (reference_id ogbench/pointmaze-large-v0), so the pixels-mode semantics are the ones that bind here

Leaves we offer that swm does not:

- `agent.scale` - rjepa's `mark_agent_red` enlarges the agent so a 6-pixel body is findable by a fixed sky camera; swm has no counterpart
- `camera.distance_scale` - swm does not expose the camera; ours is needed to reproduce the published sky-camera framings
- `camera.elevation` - as camera.distance_scale
- `floor.tint` - Multiplicative tint of the bound texture, not SWM checker RGB replacement
- `rendering.decolor_floor` - Flat slate floor, not SWM pixel_encoding=0 two-colour checker; an affine inversion alone cannot reproduce its pixels
