### The backend-agreement check cannot reach the 3-D families

`check_backend_agreement` is `not_applicable` on 4 configuration(s) for
one reason, and it is worth stating plainly rather than leaving as a status code. The check
compares the fused Triton rasterizer against the readable torch implementation of the
**same** `Scene2D`; the mjwarp, raycast3d and nvdiff families do not build a `Scene2D` at
all, so it returns `env renders without a Scene2D`. Their classes also do not offer two
interchangeable backends — `DMControlSuite` bakes a plate and ray-casts the movers,
`DMControlTrackCom` hands the whole scene to MJWarp, and an env is one or the other — so
there is no second implementation of one picture to compare against.

This is a gap in the CHECK SUITE, not a defect in a renderer, and Step 4 does not close it:
writing a new cross-backend comparison would be building the second correctness framework
section 1 forbids. It is recorded because "not applicable" on a check section 4.1 lists as
family-specific should be a fact somebody chose, not a status nobody read.

## The representation tripwire

`A = dino_cos(ours, official) / dino_cos(blank, official)`, against the bound Step 2 froze: `parity` ≤ 0.1, `visual` ≤ 0.25.

Diagnostic, and deliberately weak on its own: a cosine distance is not a task success rate and
this page does not treat it as one. It is here because it is the cheapest thing that notices a
Step 6 change that keeps every pixel metric inside its bound and still moves what the frame
means to an encoder. `ours / jpeg95` below 1 means regenerating the frame from its state lands
closer to the official renderer than storing that renderer's output as JPEG and reading it back.

## The frozen state corpora

The artifact Step 6 replays. Each corpus is drawn from the env's **own** reset-and-rollout
distribution — so every state is one the env genuinely reaches — and then selected for coverage:
the most typical row, then up to half the budget on per-dimension extremes taken widest
dimension first, then the rest by farthest-point spread. Deterministic given the seed; no
generator is consulted by the selection. Synthesising extremes instead would have produced
states the env never reaches, and a parity number on an unreachable pose describes nothing.

oracle numbers are taken at the official default appearance, which is what parity is defined

## What Step 4 does not establish

That a world model or a controller cannot tell the renderers apart. Gate A says the renderer is
internally coherent, that its fidelity declaration is supported by a measurement, that the
configuration it draws is the one the swm id registers, and that its output is reproducible.
The semantic claim is Step 5's, on PushT and Reacher, and on a visual-tier arm if a visual-tier
claim is to be advertised. Nothing here licenses it.

It also establishes nothing about speed, in either direction. Step 3 found GPU rendering broadly
healthy and CPU rendering broadly poor, with 16 configurations having no CPU renderer at all;
none of that is evidence about what the pixels are, and this page deliberately does not repeat it.
