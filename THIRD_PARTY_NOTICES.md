## Object-permanence G34/G62 reference and recorded data

`tests/fixtures/semantics/occlusion_ball/` is generated by executing the G34
nominal scene from [Hokin Deng's object-permanence](https://github.com/hokindeng/object-permanence)
at commit `824baddcdad087810d35638bf8f273b884f74975` in Blender 4.4.0.
Blender-derived poses, depth maps and masks retain the source's CC BY-NC 4.0 terms
and attribution (Copyright (c) 2026 Hokin Deng). Complete source notice and terms
are in the fixture's `SOURCE_LICENSE.txt`. Those reference data are not relicensed
under this repository's MIT license.

The runtime MuJoCo model independently describes the nominal geometry and
mathematical motion; no upstream Python source is vendored or imported at runtime.
Source identity, measured scope and regeneration are in `docs/adding/occlusion-ball.md`.
Blender is an external oracle-generation tool, not a runtime dependency.

The same source pin and attribution apply to the independently generated G62
oracle in `tests/fixtures/semantics/object_permanence/g62/nominal/` and candidate
recordings in `tests/fixtures/saved/object_permanence_rigid/`. These data carry
the source CC-BY-NC-4.0 terms, with license text in their fixture directories;
they are not relicensed under the library MIT license. Native G98/G132 source scenes under
`tests/fixtures/source/object_permanence/` are also source-derived CC-BY-NC-4.0
fixtures; their manifests bind the original scripts and native-file checksums.

## Craftax

The `craftax` family (`stable_envs/envs/craftax/`) runs
[Craftax](https://github.com/MichaelTMatthews/Craftax), which is MIT-licensed ("Copyright (c) 2024
Michael Matthews"), as an external dependency. Its simulator, renderer and textures are not copied
here; frames are the pixel observations Craftax computes. The per-lane key handling and the uint8
frame scaling follow stable-worldmodel's `stable_worldmodel/envs/craftax/craftax.py` at commit
`21446f1ede6d5284e981bd7b47f432b994e6d812` (MIT).

## SDL_gfx drawing rules

`stable_envs/render/gfxdraw/` reimplements in Torch the integer rasterization and
packed-channel alpha-blending rules of SDL_gfx by Andreas Schiffler, which pygame's
`gfxdraw` module uses. Upstream [SDL2_gfx](https://www.ferzkopp.net/wordpress/2016/01/02/sdl_gfx-sdl2_gfx/)
is licensed under the zlib license; the copy bundled in pygame (`src_c/SDL_gfx/`) is
marked LGPL. No SDL_gfx source is included.
